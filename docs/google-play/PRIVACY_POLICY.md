Brandyy ("we", "us") runs the Brandyy marketplace at https://brandyy.shop and the Brandyy mobile
apps for Android and iOS. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share
it with, and how you can control or delete it. We process data in line with Egypt's Personal
Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020).

**Controller:** [registered company name], Cairo, Egypt — support@brandyy.shop

## 1. What we collect

| Data                                                                                                                       | When                                               | Why                                                |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Name, email, password (stored hashed), phone                                                                               | You create an account or edit your profile         | Your account, sign-in, order updates               |
| Delivery addresses                                                                                                         | You add an address or check out                    | Delivering your orders                             |
| Orders and order history                                                                                                   | You place an order                                 | Fulfilling orders, returns, support, legal records |
| Payment details you provide for InstaPay / Vodafone Cash (sender number or IPA, transaction reference, receipt screenshot) | You choose a manual payment method                 | Verifying your payment                             |
| Card payments                                                                                                              | Only on the website, through our payment providers | We never see or store full card numbers            |
| Photos you upload (profile photo, product photos, payment receipts)                                                        | You choose to upload                               | Showing them where you put them                    |
| Reviews and product questions                                                                                              | You post them                                      | Shown publicly on product pages                    |
| Push notification token                                                                                                    | You allow notifications in the app                 | Sending order and account notifications            |
| Seller information (store name, pickup address and phone, bank/payout details, tax number)                                 | You apply to sell                                  | Running your store and paying you                  |
| Affiliate information (referral code, payout method)                                                                       | You join the affiliate programme                   | Tracking and paying commissions                    |
| Crash and error reports (no content of your messages or payments)                                                          | Automatically, if the app or site crashes          | Fixing bugs                                        |
| On the website only: cookies and analytics (if enabled)                                                                    | When you browse the website                        | Keeping you signed in, measuring site use          |

The mobile app does **not** collect your precise location, contacts, microphone audio, or
advertising ID, and it shows no ads.

## 2. Who we share it with

- **Sellers** receive what they need to fulfil your order: your name, delivery address, phone and
  the items ordered.
- **Couriers** receive your name, address and phone to deliver.
- **Service providers** that process data for us: hosting, image storage (Vercel Blob /
  Cloudinary), email delivery (Resend), push notifications (Expo / Google Firebase / Apple Push
  Notification service), crash reporting (Sentry), and payment providers for website card
  payments. They may only use it to provide their service to us.
- **Authorities** when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 3. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is active. Order and payment records are kept for
as long as Egyptian tax and commercial law requires, even after account deletion, but are
detached from your name and contact details where possible.

## 4. Your rights and choices

You can access and update your profile in the app (Account → Edit profile) or on the website.
You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings at any time. You can ask for a copy of
your data or object to its use by emailing support@brandyy.shop.

## 5. Deleting your account

- **In the app:** Account → Delete account (sellers and affiliates: More → Delete account).
- **On the web:** sign in and go to https://brandyy.shop/delete-account.
- Or email support@brandyy.shop from your account's email address.

Deletion removes your profile, addresses, saved items, notifications, push tokens, sign-in
sessions, payout details and uploaded receipts, and anonymises your reviews and past orders. Order
records needed for legal reasons are kept without your contact details.

## 6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. Access to personal
data is limited to staff and providers who need it.

## 7. Children

Brandyy is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

## 8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you
in the app or by email.
